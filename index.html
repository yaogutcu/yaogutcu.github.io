Active Battery Balancing System for Electric Vehicles

TÜBİTAK 2209-ABMSJul 2026

Novel dual-tier quasi-resonant active cell balancing topology for Li-ion battery packs. Achieved Zero Current Switching (ZCS), reducing peak current from 120A to ~15A and continuous current to ~6A.

Technical Details:

  • Controller: STM32F407
  • Hardware: 2-layer PCB designed in KiCad, manufactured by JLCPCB
  • Components: TI UCC21540 isolated gate driver, Mornsun B0515S DC-DC, MCP6C02 current sensor
  • Simulation: MATLAB/Simulink 2024a (Simscape). 729-scenario 3D averaged current mapping
  • Design Params: C=13.6µF, L=4.7µH, Fsw=20kHz

Advisor: Dr. Aykut Bıçak | Jury: Assoc. Prof. Davut Ertekin, R.A. Uğur Erbaş
